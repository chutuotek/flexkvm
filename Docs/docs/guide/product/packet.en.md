# Package Contents

After opening the package, verify all items are present against the checklist below. If anything is missing, contact your purchase channel or technical support.

## Standard Package

![FlexKVM Package Contents](../../images/product/package.webp)

| # | Item | Qty | Notes |
|:--:|------|:---:|-------|
| [1] | FlexKVM device | 1 | Core device |
| [2] | HDMI cable | 1 | Connects to target host's HDMI output |
| [3] | USB Type-C cable | 3 | For power, data, and ATX control. All three are identical and interchangeable, approximately 1m long, Type-A to Type-C |
| [4] | ATX controller | 1 | Remote power control for computers |
| [5] | ATX Dupont wires | 10 | ATX controller wiring, see [ATX Control](../peripherals/atx.md) |
| [6] | Magnetic backplate | 1 | Mounts device on metal surfaces (pre-installed on the back — verify it's present) |
| [7] | Quick start guide | 1 | Product usage guide |

> **Power adapter**: The device requires a separate **5V/1A power adapter** (not included). Connect via the USB Type-C cable (package item [3]). A dedicated power adapter is recommended for optimal stability.

## Extra Accessories

If you selected additional accessories at purchase, verify them against the packing slip.
